## Sales Tips Learned
1. **Preempt Layout Objections:** Ask about specific hardware locations (like battery proximity to the meter) early to prevent surprise billing complaints.
2. **Handle Trust Objections with Transparency:** Proactively admit minor installation risks (like broken tiles) to establish absolute credibility.
3. **Sell Value over Brand Prestige:** Pivot luxury brand comparisons to a financial comparison focusing on the return on investment (ROI) timeframe.
