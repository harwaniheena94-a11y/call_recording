# Sales Call Knowledge Report

## Customer Overview
- **Customer Name:** Not Mentioned
- **Location:** Postcode 2179 (Metro area near Eastern Creek)
- **Products Discussed:** 21 Solar Panels, 10 kW Inverter, 41.8 kWh & 48.7 kWh Fox ESS Batteries, Blackout Protection, NSW Home-Saver Loan.
- **Call Outcome:** Customer agreed to review the revised quote (without blackout protection) and terms before initiating a deposit.

## Call Summary
Nia from Solar Junction followed up on a solar and battery inquiry, addressing a competitor's quote from a barred NETCC entity. She offered a discounted price of $13,980 for a system with 21 panels, a 10 kW inverter, and an upgraded 48.7 kWh battery. The customer raised questions regarding battery capacities, charging times, panel layout capability, and the lack of automatic blackout protection. Nia explained the New South Wales home-saver loan financing process, which requires a minimum deposit of $200–$250. Ultimately, the customer chose to review the revised quote and terms via email before committing to a bank transfer.

## Customer Questions
- **Q: Can we fit 21 panels?** 
  - *A:* Yes, by utilizing the eastern roof as shown in the provided layout.
- **Q: How long does a 10 kW solar system take to charge the battery?** 
  - *A:* It takes approximately 4 to 5 hours during the daytime.
- **Q: Why do I need blackout protection to use the battery during a power outage?** 
  - *A:* The system is grid-tied. Without blackout protection, the battery cannot power the house when grid power goes down.
- **Q: Does the loan money go to the installer straight away?** 
  - *A:* No, the installation is completed first, and then the installer claims the funds from the state.

## Customer Concerns
- **Panel Layout:** Doubtful that 21 panels would fit.
- **Blackout Protection Cost:** Hesitant about the extra $600–$620 cost and the limitation of powering only two essential circuits.
- **Deposit Policy:** Reluctant to pay a $200–$250 deposit upfront when a competitor did not require one.

## Products & Features Discussed
- **Solar Panels:** 21 panels (utilizing northern and eastern roof faces).
- **Inverter:** 10 kW single-phase hybrid inverter.
- **Batteries:** Fox ESS stackable batteries (41.8 kWh and 48.7 kWh options).
- **Blackout Protection:** Extra feature costing ~$600 to back up two essential circuits.
- **NSW Home-Saver Loan:** 10-year interest-free financing.
- **Concrete Slab Base:** Bunnings slabs for battery placement ($150 company installation fee).

## Key Information Shared
- Solar Power Nation/One Solar is barred from the NETCC.
- A 10 kW solar system generates approximately 40 to 45 units daily.
- Loan applications require a driver's license, council rate notice, and taxation notice of assessment.

## Sales Tips Learned
- **Expose Competitor Flaws:** Highlight credentials like NETCC registration to build immediate trust over non-compliant competitors.
- **Handle Deposit Objections:** Frame the deposit as a warehouse release and active registration requirement to justify the charge.
- **Acknowledge Technical Limits Early:** Proactively explain grid-tied limitations (e.g., blackout protection) to avoid late-stage sticker shock.
