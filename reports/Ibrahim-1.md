# Sales Call Knowledge Report

## Customer Overview
- **Customer Name:** Abraham
- **Location:** Not Mentioned
- **Products Discussed:** Solar panels (16 and 18 panel options) and panel removal.
- **Call Outcome:** June agreed to review the pricing for a better offer, and Abraham agreed to send the competitor's quotation after he finishes work.

## Call Summary
June from Solar Junction called Abraham to follow up on a solar quotation sent the previous day. Abraham stated he received a competitor's quote of $10,000 for a similar system, which is lower than Solar Junction's quote of $13,500. June requested the competitor's written quotation to attempt a price match, but Abraham was initially hesitant to send it. June suggested the competitor might be a local company that is not NCC approved. Abraham explained he was currently at work but agreed to send the competitor's quotation after finishing his shift. June promised to check for a better price and call Abraham back.

## Customer Questions
- **Question:** Why I have to send you those quotes?
  - **Answer:** June explained that she needs the competitor's quote to attempt a price match.

## Customer Concerns
- **Price:** The competitor's quote of $10,000 is significantly cheaper than Solar Junction's quote of $13,500.
- **Time/Availability:** Abraham is currently at work and does not have time to send the competitor's quotation immediately.

## Products & Features Discussed
- 18 solar panels (and a 16-panel option)
- Panel removal
- Price matching
- NCC-approved status

## Key Information Shared
- Solar Junction's quotation was $13,500 (though June initially mentioned $19,800, she corrected it to $13,500).
- June noted that cheaper competitors might be local or not NCC approved.
- Abraham only needs 16 solar panels instead of the 18 panels originally discussed.

## Sales Tips Learned
1. **Request Written Quotes for Price Matches:** Always ask the customer to share the competitor's written quotation to verify details and attempt a price match.
2. **Highlight Industry Standards:** Overcome cheap competitor pricing by pointing out quality differences, such as whether the competitor is an NCC-approved company.
3. **Handle Interruptions Professionally:** When a customer is busy at work, respect their time, set a clear expectation for follow-up, and secure a commitment for them to send information later.
