# Sales Call Knowledge Report

## Customer Overview
- **Customer Name:** Ninos Israel
- **Location:** 661 Edmonton Avenue, Oslo, 2179
- **Products Discussed:** Solar panels (475W, 440W, 400W), Fox 48kW battery, Solplanet 10kW single-phase hybrid inverter, Solplanet 40.96kW battery (8 stackable units).
- **Call Outcome:** Customer emailed a competitor’s quotation to the salesperson for review and comparison.

## Call Summary
Ninos Israel called Sola Junction to request a quote for a solar and battery package. The salesperson, Martin, analyzed the customer's roof layout and determined that only 18 of the 475W panels (8.6kW) could fit, contesting a competitor's quote of 21 panels. Martin explained that an 8.6kW system generates insufficient daily power (34–35 units) to charge a 40–50kW battery while powering a home. The customer shared technical details of his competitor quote, which featured Solplanet and Ryzen equipment. Martin successfully prompted the customer to email this quote over. The call concluded with Martin receiving the email to analyze the discrepancies.

## Customer Questions
- **Question:** How much does that [18 panels of 475W] add up to?
  - **Answer:** It equals 8.6kW.
- **Question:** Is that enough to charge the battery? How long will that take to charge?
  - **Answer:** No. An 8.6kW system generates 34–35 units daily, and the home uses 10–15 units, leaving only 20–25 units, which cannot charge a 48kW battery.
- **Question:** [Regarding capacity] ...you don't use the whole thing because he does have 52 or is that the same as 50, but you just get to use 42?
  - **Answer:** No, if you have a 48kW battery, you can use the full 48kW.

## Customer Concerns
- Discrepancy between the competitor's 21-panel layout and the salesperson's 18-panel maximum.
- Whether his proposed solar array can successfully charge a large battery.
- Confusion regarding usable battery capacities and sizing.

## Products & Features Discussed
- Solar panels (475W, 440W, 400W Ryzen/Horizon Energy)
- Solplanet 10kW single-phase hybrid inverter
- Solplanet 40.96kW battery (8 stackable units)
- Fox 48kW battery
- North-facing roof installation

## Key Information Shared
- Only 18 panels of 475W (8.6kW total) can fit on the north-facing roof.
- An 8.6kW solar system generates 34–35 units daily; average household consumption uses 10–15 of those units.
- Fully utilizing a 48kW battery requires larger solar input than an 8.6kW system.
- Solplanet products are sold at Bunnings and distributed via PowerBee.

## Sales Tips Learned
1. **Verify Feasibility Live:** Use design software during the call to challenge competitor mistakes and build technical credibility.
2. **Teach the Math:** Explain generation-to-battery ratio realities so customers understand system limitations.
3. **Acquire the Competitor Quote:** Politely ask the customer to send competitor quotes to audit them for errors.
