# Sales Call Knowledge Report

## Customer Overview
- **Customer Name:** Ibrahim
- **Location:** Ingleburn, NSW 2565
- **Products Discussed:** Fox CQ7 Battery (41.8 kWh), 18x JA Solar 475W Panels (8.5 kW), 10 kW Inverter, decommissioning service
- **Call Outcome:** Quote of $13,800 ; follow-up scheduled for tomorrow.

## Call Summary
Solar Junction representative June spoke with Ibrahim about upgrading his existing 10-panel solar array. Ibrahim wants an 8.5 kW system paired with a 41.8 kWh Fox CQ7 battery on a single-phase connection. June verified property details and introduced the NSW Government’s 0% interest loan. June quoted $13,800, which includes decommissioning his old panels. Ibrahim raised a price objection, claiming a competitor quoted $10,000 for the same system. June defended the price using wholesale equipment costs and secured a follow-up call for tomorrow.

## Customer Questions
- **Can I get the NSW government loan?** Yes, Solar Junction is a NETCC-approved seller offering this 0% interest loan (up to $15,000 with up to 10 years to repay).
- **How does southern roof placement affect performance?** It reduces system efficiency.
- **What is the price of the system?** $13,800, which includes the new system and the decommissioning/removal of the old one.

## Customer Concerns
- **Competitor Price:** Received a $10,000 quote from another company.
- **Efficiency:** Loss of efficiency from placing panels on the southern roof face.
- **Communication:** Had initial difficulty understanding chronological questions.

## Products & Features Discussed
- Fox CQ7 Battery (41.8 kWh)
- JA Solar 475W Panels (16-18 panels, ~8.5 kW)
- 10 kW Single-Phase Inverter
- Decommissioning/removal of old system
- NSW Government 0% Interest-Free Loan

## Key Information Shared
- Solar Junction is a NETCC-approved solar retailer.
- Ibrahim's quarterly electricity bills are high ($600 to $900).
- The property is 29 km from the Eastern Creek warehouse.
- A 42 kWh battery alone wholesales near $10,000.

## Sales Tips Learned
1. **Leverage Finance:** Introduce interest-free loans early to minimize price friction.
2. **Logically Justify Price:** Use wholesale component costs to counter unrealistic competitor quotes.
3. **Simplify Language:** Avoid technical jargon or complex phrasing when language barriers exist.
4. **Secure Next Steps:** Always book a firm follow-up time rather than leaving it open.
