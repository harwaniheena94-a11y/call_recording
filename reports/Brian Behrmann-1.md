## Sales Tips Learned
- **Accommodate Tech Gaps:** Patiently offer alternative solutions (like calling customer support) for elderly clients struggling with digital tools.
- **Maintain Momentum:** Secure the deposit first and allow outstanding administrative documents to be collected later.
- **Disclose Surcharges Early:** Confirm the final dollar amount, including processing fees, to secure authorized consent transparently.
