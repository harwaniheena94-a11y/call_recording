# Sales Call Knowledge Report

## Customer Overview
- **Customer Name:** Calvin Rudd
- **Location:** Wellard, Western Australia (Postcode: 6170)
- **Products Discussed:** Fox ESS Battery (42kWh system), 10kW Single-Phase Inverter, blackout protection, and garage bollards.
- **Call Outcome:** Verbal agreement to purchase the 42kWh Fox ESS battery package for $11,200, funded by a $1,200 bank transfer deposit and a $10,000 finance plan.

## Call Summary
Calvin Rudd called Solar Junction seeking a 20kW solar battery to reduce high winter electricity bills caused by a broken inverter. Salesperson Paul assessed Calvin's 6.6kW system and high 30kWh daily energy usage, recommending a larger battery to support his ducted air conditioning. Paul initially quoted a 23.3kW Fox ESS battery for $8,500 but subsequently offered a 32.6kW package with blackout protection for $9,500. Ultimately, he successfully upsold Calvin to a 42kWh Fox ESS battery system for $11,200. The deal was finalized with a $1,200 deposit paid via bank transfer and a 5-year finance agreement for the remaining $10,000 through Plenty Finance.

## Key Information Shared
- High-usage properties (~30kWh/day) running air conditioning require larger battery capacities (35-40kWh) for night-time coverage.
- Plenty Finance has a maximum finance limit of $10,000.
- Quotations, product datasheets, and government recognition proofs are sent via email.

## Sales Tips Learned
- **Expose System Gaps:** Analyze actual daily usage and appliances (e.g., ducted AC) to justify upselling to a larger, more suitable battery size.
- **Use Tiered Packaging:** Bundle high-value add-ons like blackout protection and bollards for only a slight price increase to make larger sizes highly attractive.
- **Overcome Finance Caps Strategically:** Present the maximum finance limit ($10,000) as a benchmark to easily collect the remaining balance as an upfront cash deposit.
