# Sales Call Knowledge Report

## Customer Overview
- **Customer Name**: Ninos
- **Location**: Not Mentioned
- **Products Discussed**: 9.9 solar system, 41.7 kW battery, blackout protection, double-story pitched roof installation
- **Call Outcome**: Customer agreed to proceed with the updated quote once emailed, paying a $200 refundable deposit.

## Call Summary
Jeremy from Solar Function spoke with Ninos to finalize a solar quote. Ninos requested to downgrade the battery to 41.7 kW (from 48 kW) because he felt there was insufficient daylight to fully charge a larger battery while running appliances. He also requested to update the quote for a double-story house with a pitched roof, emphasizing that all safety equipment and ladder access costs must be included with no hidden fees. Jeremy agreed to include the $500 blackout protection feature and explained the parallel finance and grid approval process. Ninos agreed to transfer the $200 refundable booking deposit as soon as the revised quote was emailed.

## Customer Questions
- **Question**: With the finances, so you're going to apply, then you send me the link and I have to upload the documents?
  - **Answer**: Yes. Once the booking deposit is paid, an order confirmation is sent with a portal link and login details to upload basic documents (electricity bill, meter box picture). The team verifies these, sends a finance link to get approved, and simultaneously applies for grid distributor approval.
- **Question**: And you guys obviously don't get paid until the installation is done?
  - **Answer**: Correct. No one gets paid until the installation is completed, and the company only receives government rebates after installation details are submitted.
- **Question**: With the deposit, it was only $200?
  - **Answer**: Yes, it is $200, and it is fully refundable at any point in time.

## Customer Concerns
- **Battery Charging Sufficiency**: Concerned there is not enough daylight to run the AC and basic appliances while fully charging a 48 kW battery.
- **Hidden Charges**: Strictly wants all double-story, pitched roof, safety ropes, handrails, and ladder access equipment costs included in the upfront quote.

## Key Information Shared
- The booking deposit is fully refundable at any time.
- To prevent delays, finance approval and grid distributor approval are processed at the same time.
- Required booking documents include an electricity bill and a meter box picture.

## Sales Tips Learned
1. **Prevent Hidden Fee Anxiety**: Explicitly list all installation-related equipment (like handrails and ropes) in writing to reassure clients worried about extra fees.
2. **Value of Simultaneous Processes**: Explain back-office timelines (like parallel grid and finance approvals) to reassure the customer of an efficient timeline.
3. **Low-Risk Call to Action**: Highlight that the booking deposit is fully refundable to lower the friction of closing the deal.
