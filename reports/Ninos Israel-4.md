# Sales Call Knowledge Report

## Customer Overview
- **Customer Name:** Mia
- **Location:** New South Wales (NSW), Australia
- **Products Discussed:** Solar battery, panels, and New South Wales homes loan
- **Call Outcome:** Mia agreed to send the required loan documents as soon as she received a fresh, separate email from Ninos outlining the checklist.

## Call Summary
Mia called Ninos to address a spacing conflict regarding the proposed battery location, noting that her gate opens toward the installation site. Ninos resolved this concern by proposing to swap the positions of the gate and the fixed panel, ensuring the gate opens away from the battery. Ninos then requested the outstanding documents required to process Mia's New South Wales homes loan. Mia explained that her current email threads were disorganized, making it difficult to identify the requested items. Ninos agreed to send a dedicated, clean email listing the necessary documents. Mia promised to email these documents within ten minutes of arriving home.

## Customer Questions
- **Question:** How will the gate opening towards the battery installation area be resolved?
  - **Answer:** Ninos will swap the physical positions of the gate and the fixed panel so the gate opens on the opposite side, completely clear of the battery.
- **Question:** When was the document request email sent, and did it require filling out forms?
  - **Answer:** It was sent earlier as part of the order confirmation email. No forms need to be filled out immediately; the customer just needs to reply with the requested documents.
- **Question:** What documents are required exactly?
  - **Answer:** The required documents are an electricity bill copy, Council Rate Notice (CRN), driver's license, and Notice of Assessment (NOA).

## Customer Concerns
- **Physical Obstruction:** The gate opening radius initially overlapped with the planned battery installation location.
- **Email Clutter:** Important document requests were buried in long, continuous email threads, making them hard to find.

## Key Information Shared
- The gate and fixed panel are interchangeable to accommodate physical space constraints.
- Processing the NSW homes loan requires four specific documents: electricity bill, CRN, driver's license, and NOA.
- A new, clean quotation copy will be sent for signature only after the required documents are submitted.

## Sales Tips Learned
1. **Provide Instant Design Solutions:** Solve physical installation objections immediately by offering simple, flexible modifications (e.g., swapping panel positions).
2. **Declutter Communication:** Send critical administrative requests (like document checklists) in separate, clearly subject-lined emails rather than burying them in ongoing threads.
3. **Sequence Next Steps:** Clearly guide the customer on order of operations (documents first, updated signed contract second) to avoid confusion.
