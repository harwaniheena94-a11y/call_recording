# Sales Call Knowledge Report

## Customer Overview
- **Customer Name:** Donald Victor Williams (Donald V. Williams)
- **Location:** New South Wales, Australia
- **Products Discussed:** 9.5 kW Solar System (20 panels, upgraded 10 kW single-phase inverter), 30 kWh battery, blackout protection, interest-free financing.
- **Call Outcome:** Customer paid a $200 deposit (plus a $2.80 surcharge) to finalize the purchase of the $9,591 system and agreed to submit documents via the online portal.

## Call Summary
Don called Jeremy to finalize his solar system design and chose a 20-panel option with an extra battery. Because the standard 5 kW inverter could not support 20 panels, Jeremy secured a manager's approval to upgrade Don to a 10 kW inverter at no extra charge. They discussed technical specifications, including the linear degradation warranty of the panels and lithium-ion battery lifespans. Don expressed frustration that the system requires an additional $800 blackout protection package to run during grid outages. Jeremy explained that hybrid systems shut down during blackouts to protect the grid unless specific circuits are isolated. Don ultimately paid the deposit over the phone and agreed to upload his financing documents to the customer portal.

## Customer Questions
- **Q: What does linear performance warranty mean?**
  - **A:** Panels degrade by 1% in the first year, and then by 0.4% annually from year 2 to year 30. If degradation exceeds this, you can claim the warranty.
- **Q: What is the warranted capacity of the panels after 30 years?**
  - **A:** The guaranteed efficiency is approximately 87.4% after 30 years.
- **Q: What happens if there is a blackout with the current quote?**
  - **A:** Without the $800 blackout protection, the hybrid system shuts down entirely when the grid goes dark.
- **Q: Can we add the blackout protection later?**
  - **A:** Yes, it can be added at any point before the installation occurs.

## Customer Concerns
- The system shutting down during blackouts despite having a 30 kWh battery.
- Inverter capacity limits for handling the extra panels.

## Products & Features Discussed
- 20 solar panels (9.5 kW system)
- ESY Sunroom HM 10 kW single-phase hybrid inverter
- 30 kWh battery
- Blackout protection package ($800 extra)
- Government rebates and interest-free loans

## Key Information Shared
- Rebates are pre-applied to the quote, reducing the system cost from $17,000 to $9,591.
- Batteries degrade by 0.5% to 0.8% annually, retaining 60% to 70% capacity at year 10.
- Inverter warranties are 10 years; connecting a new inverter to an old system can void future product warranties.

## Sales Tips Learned
- Leverage free equipment upgrades (e.g., upgrading to a 10 kW inverter) to secure customer commitment.
- Set realistic expectations about product lifespans (e.g., 15 years) rather than relying solely on 30-year theoretical warranties.
- Validate the customer's logic during objections (such as the blackout protection cost) while maintaining technical accuracy.
