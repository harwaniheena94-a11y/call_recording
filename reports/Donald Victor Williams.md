# Sales Call Knowledge Report

## Customer Overview
- **Customer Name:** Don
- **Location:** Woodford, NSW 2778
- **Products Discussed:** 7.6 kW Solar Panels, 5 kW Inverter, Sun Home & Fox CSS Batteries, VPP, Blackout Protection
- **Call Outcome:** Don agreed to review the emailed quotes and government financing links over 48 hours to finalize the contract.

## Call Summary
Jeremy discussed solar sizing options with Don. Based on Don’s 13 kWh/day usage and a future pool, Jeremy recommended a 7.6 kW system. He compared the budget Fox CSS battery with the premium AI-powered Sun Home battery (20 or 30 kWh), highlighting Sun Home’s superior after-sales support. Jeremy explained Virtual Power Plants (VPP) and clarified that blackout protection costs $800 extra to wire two circuits. Lastly, he introduced an NSW interest-free loan, which Don agreed to review alongside the emailed quotes.

## Customer Questions
- **Q: Does panel count relate to battery size?** A: The correct way is matching solar generation capacity to total daily consumption.
- **Q: How does VPP work?** A: Retailers draw electricity from the battery during peak demand, paying $0.40–$1.00/kWh.
- **Q: What is the loan interest rate?** A: 0% interest on a 10-year NSW government loan.
- **Q: Are rebates subject to government approval?** A: No, rebates are fixed and claimed post-installation; only distributor (Endeavour) approval is required.

## Customer Concerns
- **VPP Complexity:** Felt negotiating contracts with retailers seemed complicated.
- **Blackout Protection Cost:** Hesitant about paying $800 extra when expecting the battery to run the whole house automatically.
- **Financing Eligibility:** Worried his age (78) and lack of tax returns would disqualify him.

## Key Information Shared
- Clean Energy Council averages 4 peak generation hours daily in Australia.
- Solar energy priority: 1. House, 2. Battery, 3. Grid.
- System warranties: 30 years (panels), 10 years (inverter/battery).
- VPP sign-up bonuses range from $400 to $800.

## Sales Tips Learned
- **Consultative Sizing:** Upsell based on future load additions (e.g., swimming pools).
- **Value Selling:** Discredit cheap competitors by focusing on after-sales support and AI features.
- **Finance Objection Handling:** Pitch government interest-free loans to bypass budget barriers.
