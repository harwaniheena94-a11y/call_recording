## Sales Tips Learned
1. **Remain Patient During Payment Failures:** Thoroughly re-verify details (spelling, CVV, exp date) step-by-step to salvage a declining transaction.
2. **Set Clear Post-Call Timelines:** Inform the customer exactly when to expect emails (e.g., "in the next 10 minutes") to build trust.
3. **Upsell Referrals on Positive Milestones:** Leverage a successful transaction to introduce referral incentives.
