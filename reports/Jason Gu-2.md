# Sales Call Knowledge Report

## Customer Overview
- **Customer Name:** Jason (Card Name: Qigu)
- **Location:** New South Wales (NSW)
- **Products Discussed:** 48 kWh Solar Battery, Inverter, 6.6 kW Solar Panels
- **Call Outcome:** Customer paid a $300 deposit (plus 1.4% bank surcharge) on the call to secure a discounted battery system for $13,300.

## Call Summary
The salesperson called Jason to finalize a solar battery upgrade purchase. They negotiated the price of a 48 kWh battery system down to $13,300 (from a standard $16,500) because Jason is an existing customer. The salesperson explained that the remaining balance could be funded interest-free over 10 years using the NSW government’s Home Energy Saver Loan. Jason learned his old inverter would be returned to him to sell independently. He also mentioned three friends with 10-year-old solar systems, and the salesperson advised they should do a full replacement. Ultimately, Jason agreed to pay the $300 booking deposit over the phone to secure the discounted price.

## Customer Questions
- **Q:** How much is the 48 kWh battery?
  - **A:** It costs $14,500, but is discounted to $13,300 for you.
- **Q:** What happens to my old inverter?
  - **A:** It will be replaced, and the old one will be given back to you to sell.
- **Q:** Can your company buy my old inverter?
  - **A:** No, but you can sell it on platforms like eBay.
- **Q:** Do my friends have to change their 10-year-old solar systems?
  - **A:** Yes, because 10 years ago the panels only had 10-year warranties, which have now expired.
- **Q:** Are there any account fees on the finance, like $10 a month?
  - **A:** No.

## Customer Concerns
- Price matching with other competitor offers.
- Wanting the company to buy back his old inverter.
- Hesitation to pay the deposit on the phone before receiving a confirmation email.

## Products & Features Discussed
- 48 kWh solar battery and inverter
- 6.6 kW solar panel systems (for referrals)
- NSW Home Energy Saver Loan (interest-free government finance up to 10 years, up to $15,000)
- $300 booking deposit (plus 1.4% bank card surcharge)

## Key Information Shared
- The standard price for the system is $16,500, but booking immediately secured a $13,300 rate.
- Old inverters are returned to customers to sell independently on platforms like eBay.
- The interest-free finance program is a NSW government scheme, not a company program.

## Sales Tips Learned
1. **Create Urgency:** Emphasize that special discounts require an immediate booking deposit to hold stock and secure pricing.
2. **Leverage Customer History:** Reference the customer's prior relationship to build trust and overcome hesitation about making over-the-phone payments.
3. **Capture Referral Leads:** Actively encourage existing customers to refer friends who have outdated, out-of-warranty solar systems.
