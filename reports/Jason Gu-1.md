# Sales Call Knowledge Report

## Customer Overview
- **Customer Name:** Jason
- **Location:** 26 Muller Crescent
- **Products Discussed:** Fox CSS 10 kW hybrid inverter, 28 kWh battery
- **Call Outcome:** Customer was provided with product recommendations, total pricing ($11,500), and government financing details.

## Call Summary
Nick from Solar Junction called Jason regarding his inquiry about adding a battery. Jason requested a battery from the same company that provided his existing solar panels. Based on Jason's electricity bill of around $300, Nick recommended a 28 kWh battery. Nick explained that Jason's current inverter must be upgraded to a 10 kW Fox CSS hybrid inverter to support the battery. The total fully installed price is $11,500 after government rebates, with interest-free government financing options available.

- A 28 kWh battery is suitable for properties with electricity bills of approximately $300.

## Sales Tips Learned
1. **Leverage Brand Loyalty:** Welcome repeat customers enthusiastically when they request matching system components.
2. **Qualify Size by Bill Value:** Use the customer’s average electricity bill to quickly estimate and recommend the correct battery capacity.
3. **Handle Technical Obstacles Proactively:** Clearly explain necessary hardware upgrades (e.g., swapping to a hybrid inverter) before quoting prices.
4. **Soften Cost with Finance Options:** Immediately follow high-ticket pricing with rebates and interest-free payment terms to reduce price friction.
